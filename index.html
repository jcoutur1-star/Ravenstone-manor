Before the first toast

One of you murdered Cheryl. The murderer must keep their means, motive, and opportunity hidden while steering suspicion elsewhere.

Each guest receives four private clues. Compare notes, mark your suspect matrix, and inspect one clue for tampering. You may share evidence with up to two guests.

The discussion lasts up to ten minutes. Two guests can call for a vote early; if they do, any two guests can postpone it. The investigation still closes at ten minutes.
